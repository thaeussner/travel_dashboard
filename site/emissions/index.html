How the comparison is built

Reading this page

Switches vs. ranges

The switches are choices: equally defensible ways of counting that give different answers. They are never blended into an error bar. The ranges are uncertainty within one way of counting: factors, engine loads, load factors and the size of aviation's non-CO₂ effect, sampled together.

Fair comparison

Every switch applies to actual travel and alternatives alike. "As observed" only changes travel where a head count was entered; alternatives that never happened use typical loads. The alternatives are the realistic options chosen by hand for the same destinations.
