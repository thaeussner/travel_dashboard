Factors

  • Scope: producing the fuel or electricity plus burning it. Building vehicles and infrastructure is left out.
  • Trains: each country's grid mix, not green certificates. Renfe's certificates would give 3.8, the low end of its range.
  • Flights: fuel per flight for the aircraft class. With “+ non-CO₂” the CO₂ is multiplied by about 1.7 (1.2–3) for contrails and NOx. Only flights get this.
  • Ships: the ship's own fuel per nautical mile from EU reports, split by the ferry share below. Soot and sulphate effects are left out, as in ISO 14083.
  • Cars and sailing: logged litres beat any factor (grade A). A yacht's engine hours count 1.2–4 L/h (grade B); add half an hour per hour of dinghy use.
  • Occupancy: the factors are at typical loads. An entered head count rescales them.

Grades

A
Fuel measured for this trip: litres refuelled for a car or logged on a boat.
B
This vessel, service or trip: a ship's own fuel from EU reports, a yacht's engine hours.
C
Operator, model or country: TGV, Renfe, a small petrol car, an aircraft class.
D
Generic for the mode, e.g. "local bus". Worth researching if it matters.
